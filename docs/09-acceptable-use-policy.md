# Acceptable Use Policy: CHMart

> **Document ID:** GRC-10 | **Framework:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 | **Owner:** IT Operations Manager | **Status:** Approved Baseline

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines acceptable and prohibited uses of **CHMart**'s information systems, corporate devices, cloud platforms, and data assets to protect the organization and its customers from malware, data leakage, and legal liability.

---

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all 185 **CHMart** employees (office and remote), interns, contractors, and temporary personnel using company-issued hardware (laptops, workstations, network equipment) or accessing corporate cloud platforms (**Microsoft 365**, **AWS**, **Shopify Plus**, **Stripe**, **GitHub Enterprise**).

---

## 3. Policy Statements

| Topic | Mandatory Policy Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Authorized Business Use** | Corporate devices and accounts are provided primarily for CHMart business operations. Limited, incidental personal browsing is permitted provided it does not consume excessive bandwidth, interfere with duties, or violate security rules. |
| **Endpoint & Remote Work Security** | Remote and office staff must use only MDM-enrolled, corporate-managed devices for work. Disabling EDR agents, full-disk encryption (BitLocker/FileVault), OS updates, or automatic screen locks (max **5 minutes** idle) is strictly prohibited. |
| **Data Handling & Sharing** | Restricted and Confidential data (customer PII, payment logs, HR records, proprietary source code) must never be transferred to personal email accounts, unmanaged personal devices (BYOD), public AI tools, or unapproved USB drives. SharePoint external links must require explicit authentication and expiration dates. |
| **Software & Cloud Services** | Employees shall not install unauthorized software, browser extensions, or unapproved third-party Shopify/SaaS integrations ("Shadow IT") without prior IT and Information Security approval. |
| **Network & Wi-Fi Usage** | Remote staff must connect via corporate VPN/Zero-Trust gateways when accessing internal resources on public or home networks. Corporate office guests must use only the isolated Guest Wi-Fi network. |
| **Prohibited Activities** | Users must not attempt to bypass security controls, conduct unauthorized vulnerability scanning, share corporate credentials, distribute copyrighted/illegal material, or use company resources for external commercial gain. |

---

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
| :--- | :--- |
| **IT Operations Manager** | Maintains endpoint security baselines, approves software/SaaS requests, and monitors compliance across corporate devices. |
| **Information Security Lead** | Oversees data loss prevention (DLP) rules, web filtering, and acceptable use investigations. |
| **Human Resources (HR)** | Ensures all personnel sign the Acceptable Use acknowledgment during onboarding and enforces disciplinary actions when required. |
| **All Personnel** | Complies with all acceptable use rules, protects assigned hardware from theft or loss, and reports lost devices immediately. |

---

## 5. Enforcement
**CHMart** reserves the right to monitor corporate networks, cloud logs, and managed endpoints in accordance with applicable employment laws. Violations of this policy may result in revocation of system access, formal HR disciplinary action up to termination of employment, and civil or criminal prosecution where applicable.
